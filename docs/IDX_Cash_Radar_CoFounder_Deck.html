02 / THE PROBLEM

Why Everyday Investors & Novice Traders Get Trapped on IDX.

12M+ Indonesian retail investors have charting apps, yet still get burned by sudden debt restructurings and "paper profit" stocks.

1. "PAPER PROFIT" TRAP
A company reports positive Net Income, so beginners think it's healthy, unaware that Operating Cash Flow (CFO) is negative and cash is stuck in uncollected receivables.
2. PR HEADLINE BIAS
Bullish news ("Wins IDR 5.6T Contract") triggers FOMO buying, even when the company's bank cash covers less than 20% of its maturing short-term debt.
3. PDF & RATIO OVERLOAD
Official IDX financial statements are 150+ pages long, while generic AI chatbots hallucinate financial numbers when asked to summarize them.
The Gap: Retail traders need one place that translates audited XBRL cash health into plain language alongside live price charts and daily news.
